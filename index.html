A Discord bot for Shadowdark RPG guilds

Your table's sheets, loot and lore, inside Discord.

Shadowdark Bot keeps every player's character sheet and the guild's shared stuff in one place: the stash, the coffers, the magic item treasury, the spell list and the creatures you've met. Use it with slash commands in any channel, or open the full sheet as a Discord Activity.

Set it up Commands See the Activity

⚔Character sheets

Stats, abilities, spells, effects, gold and gear slots for every player, editable by the owner.

⌂Guild storage

Shared stashes with gear-slot capacity. Anyone can donate, and anyone with a character can take.

✦Magic item treasury

Magic items are borrowed and returned, never given away. Everyone can see who has what.

◉Coffers & catalog

One shared purse in gp/sp/cp and an item catalog to buy from, priced the way you set it.

☠Spells & creatures

The full Shadowdark spell reference, plus a bestiary of what the party has met and when.

★Dungeon Master mode

Whoever holds your DM role can open and edit any player's sheet.

Setup

Get the bot into your server

You need a Discord application, somewhere to run the bot, and a few settings. The bot alone gives you every slash command. The web sheet and the Activity are optional extras.

  1. Create the Discord app

    • Open the Discord Developer Portal, click New Application and name it.
    • Under Bot, leave every privileged intent off, then Reset Token and copy the token. That's your DISCORD_TOKEN. Keep it secret.
    • Under OAuth2 → URL Generator, tick the bot and applications.commands scopes. Then tick the Send Messages, Embed Links, Read Message History and Use Slash Commands permissions. Open the URL it gives you and pick your server.

    No server ID or role ID is needed for the basics: the bot finds every server it's in and registers its commands there when it starts.

  2. Run the bot

    On Proxmox (recommended): one command on the Proxmox host makes a small Debian container with Docker, clones the bot, asks for your token and starts it.

    bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bunnyUFO/shadow-dark-bot/main/scripts/install-proxmox.sh)

    Anywhere with Docker:

    git clone https://github.com/bunnyUFO/shadow-dark-bot.git
cd shadow-dark-bot
cp .env.example .env        # then set DISCORD_TOKEN
docker compose up -d --build

    When the log shows Synced N command(s) to guild …, type /ping in your server. It answers pong. The full install, update and backup guide is in docs/deploy-proxmox.md.

  3. Settings

    Everything lives in the bot's .env file (see .env.example). Restart the bot after a change.

    SettingWhat it's for
    DISCORD_TOKENRequired. The bot token from step 1.
    LOCAL_WEB_ENABLEDtrue serves the sheet on your home network (port 8081). There's no login: you pick your character.
    WEB_ENABLEDtrue serves the public sheet with Discord login (port 8080, behind your reverse proxy). This is what the Activity uses.
    PUBLIC_BASE_URL, DISCORD_CLIENT_ID, DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET, SESSION_SECRETNeeded for the public sheet: its address, the app's OAuth2 ID and secret, and a long random string that signs logins.
    ALLOWED_GUILD_IDSServer IDs whose members may log in to the public sheet.
    FORWARDED_ALLOW_IPSYour reverse proxy's IP, so the public sheet trusts its headers. Never *.
    DM_ROLE_IDSDiscord role IDs whose holders are Dungeon Masters.

  4. Turn on the Activity

    The Activity is the web sheet opened inside Discord. Once the public sheet is running at your address:

    • Activities → enable. This adds the Launch entry point.
    • URL Mappings → map / to your sheet's domain.
    • OAuth2 → add the redirect https://<your domain>/auth/callback so the standalone website can log in too.
    • App Testers → invite each player. Each player has to accept the invite.

    Heads up: an unverified Activity launches only for the app's owner and up to 50 App Testers, and only in servers with fewer than 25 members. The standalone website at your domain works everywhere.

  5. Choose your Dungeon Masters

    Give the role your DMs already have (for example game master) edit rights over every sheet:

    • In Discord, turn on Developer Mode (User Settings → Advanced).
    • Open Server Settings → Roles, right-click the role and choose Copy Role ID.
    • Add it to .env and restart the bot:
    DM_ROLE_IDS=123456789012345678

    List several roles separated by commas. Giving or removing the role takes effect at that person's next login to the sheet. In Discord it's checked on every button press, so it takes effect at once.

  6. Try it without Discord

    The repo has a dev runner that serves the sheet on a seeded sample party. It needs no token and never touches the real database:

    py -3.12 -m venv .venv && source .venv/Scripts/activate
pip install -e ".[dev]"
python -m shadowdark_bot.dev --reset --dm 900000000000000001

    Open http://localhost:8081 and pick a character. --dm makes Brannoc a Dungeon Master so you can try DM mode.

Commands

Slash commands

Every reply is visible only to you, with a Share with channel button to post a public copy. The guild commands each have a browse view where you can list, inspect and act in one place. Start there if you're not sure which command to run.

/character Your own player character

/character sheet member?

Opens your interactive sheet with three tabs:

  • Combat: HP, AC, abilities, spellcasting, proficiencies and spells, with Manage Spells.
  • Inventory: gold, carried items and your stash, with Manage Held, Manage Stash and Give.
  • Roleplaying: identity, background, languages, talents, and Delete.

If you don't have a character yet, a Create character button starts one. DM Dungeon Masters can name a member to edit that player's character instead.

e.g. /character sheet · /character sheet member:@Tessa
/character carry item quantity? gear_slots?

Adds an item you're carrying. A catalog name links the item. Any other name makes a freeform item, and gear_slots sets how many slots each one takes. Capacity is max(10, STR) gear slots.

e.g. /character carry item:Arrows quantity:20
/character show member

Shows another player's sheet, read-only. Only their held items are listed; the stash stays private. Dropdowns let you inspect a spell or an item in place.

e.g. /character show member:@Tessa

/items The guild's item catalog

Every item has a type (common, weapon, armor, scroll, potion, loot, crafted or magical), gear slots and a bundle size. For example, 20 arrows fit in one slot.

/items browse type?

Lists the catalog, grouped by type. Pick an item to see it, then press Edit to change it.

/items add name type description? gear_slots? bundle_size? gp? sp? cp?

Adds a new item to the catalog.

e.g. /items add name:Arrows type:common bundle_size:20 sp:1
/items info name · /items edit name type? · /items remove name

Look up an item, edit it in a pre-filled form, or remove it if nothing uses it any more.

/inventory Shared, non-magical stashes

/inventory browse location?

Pick a location, then an item. + Add more and − Take work right there; taking puts the item in your character's held items.

/inventory add location item quantity? notes?

Puts a catalog item into a location. Same items stack, and capacity is checked bundle by bundle.

e.g. /inventory add location:"Main Stash" item:Arrows quantity:15
/inventory location-create · location-edit · location-delete

Make, resize or remove storage locations, such as a main stash or a lakeside cabin, each with its own gear-slot capacity.

/coffers The guild's shared purse

/coffers browse

Shows the balance and a dropdown of catalog items that have a price. Pick one and a quantity to buy it with guild money.

/coffers add gp? sp? cp? reason? · /coffers subtract gp? sp? cp? reason?

Pay in or take out. The balance can't go below zero.

e.g. /coffers add gp:120 reason:"dungeon haul"

/treasury Magic items, borrowed and returned

/treasury browse status?

Lists available and borrowed items. Pick one to Borrow for me, borrow for someone else, or Return it. A borrowed item also shows up in the borrower's carried gear.

/treasury add item tag? · /treasury remove entry_id

Registers a magic item. Each physical copy is tracked separately, and tag tells duplicates apart. You can only remove items that aren't borrowed.

/spells The spell reference

/spells browse spell_class? tier? alignment? name?

Filter every Tier 1–5 wizard and priest spell, then open one for its duration, range and text.

/spells info spell

Jumps straight to one spell, with autocomplete.

e.g. /spells info spell:"Magic Missile"
Activity

The full sheet, inside Discord

Start the Activity from Discord's App Launcher in any channel, or open the same sheet as a website in your browser. It signs you in with Discord, so you always land on your own character. On phones the sections move to a tab bar at the bottom.

Character sheet

Your sheet in three tabs. Every card has its own Edit, and changes save straight away.

  • Combat:
    • HP, AC (base + DEX), level and spellcasting, with an XP bar and a +XP button.
    • The six abilities and your proficiencies.
    • Spells: tick a spell off when it's lost, then Rest to get them back. Learn and Forget spells here too.
    • Effects: things like Light or Bless, with round boxes to tick down.
  • Inventory: gold, the items you carry (max(10, STR) gear slots) and your 10-slot stash. Open an item to Stash/Take it, Give it to someone, Drop it or Edit it.
  • Roleplaying: background, languages, talents and anything else worth remembering.

Party

Everyone's characters at a glance. Open one to read their sheet: the same tabs, read-only. Only their held items are listed; stashes stay private.

Guild

Everything the guild shares:

  • The coffers.
  • Every storage location and what's in it. Take an item into your carried gear or stash.
  • The magic item treasury, showing who has each item. Borrow one for yourself, another character or anyone by name, and Return it, whoever borrowed it.

Items

The guild's catalog, with a search box and a type filter. Buy an item with your own gold or the guild coffers, and put it in your gear, your stash or a guild location. An item that causes an effect, like a potion, links to it.

Spells

The complete Shadowdark spell list. Filter by class, tier and alignment, or search by name, then open a spell for its duration, range and full text. Spells that cause an effect link to it, ready to apply to a character.

Creatures

A bestiary of what the party has met. Each creature has a Shadowdark-style stat block: AC, HP, level, attacks, movement, alignment and ability modifiers. Anything you haven't found out yet shows as ?. Log each encounter with its date, session, place and what happened. Anyone can add, edit or delete.

The effects library sits alongside. It holds reusable effects with a duration and stat changes, each linked to the items, spells and creatures that cause it. Apply one to yourself or a party member and it lands on their Combat tab.

DM mode DM

Anyone holding a role in DM_ROLE_IDS is a Dungeon Master (see Setup). Everyone else edits only their own character.

  • In the Activity: open a player from Party to get their whole sheet, stash included, with every edit button. A banner says whose sheet you're editing.
  • In Discord: /character sheet member:@player opens that player's interactive sheet for you. Anyone else who tries is politely refused.
  • DMs can change anything on an existing character, including deleting it. They can't create a character for someone who hasn't made one.
